PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Meet the NeuroStep AI Smart Insole

More than an insole - a smarter way to understand movement.

ARCHITECTURE

Smart Sensing Architecture

Integrated high-density pressure mapping, 6-axis motion sensing, low-power processing, and wireless streaming.

⚡

Pressure Sensor Grid

Piezoresistive force array mapping regional plantar pressure across heel, midfoot, and forefoot.

🎯

Motion Sensing IMU

6-axis inertial measurement tracking acceleration, angular velocity, and stride kinetics.

🧠

Embedded Processing

Ultra-low power microcontroller unit (MCU) executing real-time signal filtering and conditioning.

📡

Wireless Streaming

Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) transceiver streaming continuous gait data to mobile devices.
